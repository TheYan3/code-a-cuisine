In short

Code à Cuisine sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising tools. You don't need an account. Fonts and images are served from this website's own server. Personal data is only processed where it is technically necessary, as described below.

Hosting and server logs

This website runs on a server in Germany operated by the controller (nginx behind a Traefik reverse proxy). When you open a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested address and your browser identification. This data is processed only to deliver the website and to keep it secure and running; it is not used to identify you or combined with other data. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working, secure website).

Generating recipes

When you press "Generate a recipe", your entries — ingredients with amounts and units, portions, number of cooks, cooking time, cuisine and diet — are sent to an automation server (n8n) operated by the controller at flow.uniquehomebase.de. It turns them into a text prompt and sends that prompt to the Gemini API of Google, which writes the recipes. The prompt contains only these entries, not your IP address or any other identifier.

Under the Gemini API terms, users in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom get the data terms of Google's paid services even on the free quota: Google does not use the prompts or the generated recipes to improve its products, processes them under its data processing addendum and logs them only for a limited time to detect misuse.

To limit each visitor to three generations per day, the n8n server reads your IP address from the request, also when the generator shows how many generations you have left. Before anything is stored, the address is turned into a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256 with a secret key that exists only on the n8n server). Only this hash, together with the number of generations made from it that day, is stored in the Firebase database described below — your IP address itself is not. All counters of a day are deleted automatically a few minutes after midnight (German time) at the end of that day. They are not visible to other visitors. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in protecting the recipe generation from abuse).

For troubleshooting, the n8n server keeps a log of the requests it processes, including technical request data such as your IP address. These log entries are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Public recipe library (Firebase)

Every generated recipe is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database (data center region europe-west1) and can be viewed by anyone in the public library. A stored recipe contains its title, cuisine, diet, cooking time, portions, number of cooks, ingredients, steps, nutrition estimates, creation time and number of likes — no name, no IP address and no other information about who generated it. The ingredient names you enter can appear in the published recipe, so please don't enter personal information there.

When you open the library or a recipe, your browser loads the recipes directly from Firebase, so Google receives your IP address as part of that connection. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the library).

Likes

Liking a recipe raises its like counter in Firebase by one, directly from your browser; nothing identifying you is stored with it. So that the same browser can't like a recipe twice, the ids of the recipes you liked are kept in your browser's local storage ("code-a-cuisine.liked-recipes"). They never leave your device, and you can remove them at any time by clearing this website's data in your browser.

Browser storage while generating

While you go through the generator, your entries are kept in your browser's session storage ("code-a-cuisine.recipe-request") so a reload doesn't lose them. They stay on your device and are deleted when you close the tab. Neither this nor the local storage above is used for tracking; both are strictly necessary for the functions you use.
